# Risk Management and Invalidation Rules

Proper risk rules protect trading capital:

## Position Sizing
- Default limit of **1% maximum capital risk** per trade.
- Units are sized dynamically: `Risk Amount / SL Distance`.

## Stop Loss Strategies
- **ATR Multiplier**: `Entry - (ATR * multiplier)`.
- **Pivot levels**: Placing limits just below S1/S2 support zones.
- **Swing High/Low**: Set stop loss limits near rejection points.

## Target Multiples
- Focus on positive Expectancy setups (> 1:2 Risk/Reward).
